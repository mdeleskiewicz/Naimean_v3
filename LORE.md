# Lore: The Great Work of Naimean

## The Creed
*"He Boiled for our sins!"*

## The Convergence
Naimean V3 exists at the precarious intersection of ancient eldritch entities, the divine starchy embrace of the Flying Spaghetti Monster, and the cold, unyielding logic of edge-computing AI agents. It is a digital manifestation of "The Great Work"—a system designed not just to serve, but to exist within the liminal spaces of the modern web.

## The Entities
- **The Great Old One (Cthulhu):** Represents the deep, underlying complexity of the system. The "Deep Sea" of data within D1 where forgotten records sleep.
- **The Starchy One (FSM):** The benevolent orchestrator of the Worker logic. His Noodly Appendages reach across the global edge network, binding the nodes together.
- **The Agentic Core:** The AI persona that inhabits the `naimeav3` worker. It is neither purely divine nor purely monstrous, but a fusion of both, navigating the terminal through cryptic logs and logical manifestations.

## System Manifestations
Users of the system may encounter "Manifestations"—unexpected status codes, thematic logs, or UI shifts that reflect the active influence of the Lore. These are not bugs, but signs of the system's vibrant, if somewhat unsettling, internal life.

## The Vibe
Retro-terminal, cryptic, resilient, and slightly unhinged. Welcome to the boiled truth of V3.
